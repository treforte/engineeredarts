Ameca, answered

Ameca is Engineered Arts' flagship social humanoid robot — the name and face of embodied AI. It holds real conversations, expresses in real time across 32 degrees of freedom, speaks 70+ languages, and can run autonomous AI workflows through the Tritium software suite.

Ameca is the full-size flagship. The Desktop line — Ameca Desktop, Ami and Azi — brings the same Gen 3 perception, expression and AI stack into a compact, countertop form that deploys in minutes. Same platform, different footprint and embodiment.

Yes — Ameca is available to rent for events, expos and activations, so you can create the moment without owning the robot. Tell us your dates in the demo request and we'll walk you through it.
